Who in your organisation can help?

Thank you for joining the Federation Day. This is the online version of the theme carousel posters. For each DURF activity, tell us who in your organisation could help with the in-kind contributions we ask for, in which role, and how we can reach them.

Takes about 15 minutes. Your answers are saved in this browser as you type, so you can stop and come back later. Nothing is sent until you download your answers and email them to us (step 4). Fill in only what applies; empty activities are fine.
